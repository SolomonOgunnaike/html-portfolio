About Me

Hi, I’m Solomon Ogunnaike, based in Lagos, Nigeria. I work at the point where customer service, technology and attention to detail meet.

I’ve spent time on the front line serving customers in retail and food service, handling orders, payments and enquiries in busy, high-volume settings. That work taught me how to stay calm under pressure, communicate clearly and make sure people leave satisfied.

On the technical side, I build with full-stack web development, support WordPress websites by sourcing and selecting images that fit their content, and do AI data annotation, where accuracy and careful judgment matter most.

I’m currently studying Building Engineering/Technology at the University of Lagos, and I’m always learning new tools and ways of working. I’m open to remote and project-based opportunities where I can combine people skills with digital skills.
